# Economy — 60 days, 10 troops

`node tools/economy.js --days 60 --troops 10` · version 1.20.0

Unchanged by 1.20.0: the "sell any good" change is a player-facing market filter and does not
affect these playerless income curves. An idle or trading army with no fief still bleeds out as
its troops starve; a fief holder compounds steadily upward.
